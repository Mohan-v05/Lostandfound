About UVA FindIt

The Problem

Uva Wellassa University frequently faces issues where students and staff lose personal and academic items such as ID cards, certificates, books, electronic devices, and bags within the campus. Currently, there is no centralized digital platform to report or recover such items, leading to frustration and permanent loss of valuable belongings.

Our Objective

UVA FindIt aims to provide a centralized, user-friendly digital platform to report lost items, submit found items, and help rightful owners recover their belongings efficiently. By bridging the gap between finders and losers, we foster a culture of honesty and cooperation within the university community.

Scope of the Application

  • Reporting lost items with detailed descriptions and campus locations.
  • Reporting found items to notify the potential owner.
  • Searching and filtering items for quick discovery.
  • Direct contact between the finder and the owner.
  • Internal dashboard for system management.
