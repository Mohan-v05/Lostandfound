Welcome to UVA FindIt! 👋

Learn how to use the Item Details page to find your lost items or help return found items to their owners.

What is the Item Details Page?

The Item Details page shows complete information about a specific lost or found item. When you click on any item from the main list, you'll see all its details including a photo, description, location, contact information, and more. It's your one-stop place to get all the information you need about an item!

How to Use This Page - Step by Step

1

Go to Items List

First, visit the "All Items" page from the navigation menu. You'll see a grid of all lost and found items on campus.

💡 Tip: You can browse by category or use filters to find what you're looking for!

2

Click on an Item Card

Find an item you're interested in and click on its card. This will take you to the detailed page with all the information about that specific item.

💡 Tip: Look at the status badge - it shows if item is "Lost" or "Found"!

3

Review the Item Details

Now you'll see the complete item information including:

  • Item Photo/Image - Visual of the item
  • Item Name - What is it?
  • Category - Type (Electronics, Documents, etc.)
  • Location - Where it was lost/found
  • Date - When it was reported
  • Description - Details about the item
4

Contact the Owner

You'll find the contact number on the details page. If you found a matching item or recognize your lost item, you can call or text the person using this number.

💡 Tip: Be polite and clear when describing why you're contacting them!

5

Mark Item as Recovered (Optional)

If you've successfully recovered the item, you can upload a recovery proof image and mark it as recovered. This helps confirm the item has been reunited!

💡 Tip: Take a photo of you with the item for proof!

Important Information

  • Privacy First: Never share personal information on public posts. Contact the person privately.
  • Be Honest: Only report items you genuinely lost or found. False reports can be misleading.
  • Be Respectful: When contacting someone, be polite and professional.
  • Safety: Meet in public places and bring a friend when recovering items.
  • Proof of Ownership: Be prepared to describe specific details of the item to prove it's yours.
